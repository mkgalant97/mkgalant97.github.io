Welcome

Music, mountains, and everything in between

Pianist Misha Galant is the 2025 winner of the National Federation of Music Clubs Young Artist Competition and a 2020 Gilmore Young Artist. He has performed with the Fort Worth Symphony, Rochester Philharmonic, and Utah Symphony, and makes his home in Salt Lake City, where the Wasatch mountains are never far from the practice room.
